# NotIkea

**A marketplace for refurbished furniture, with AR and AI to help you place it.**

## Project Description

NotIkea is an iOS app where people can buy refurbished furniture and sell their old, unused furniture instead of throwing it away.

- **Marketplace:** list furniture you no longer use, and browse second-hand pieces from others.
- **AR preview:** see how a piece of furniture looks in your own room before you buy it.
- **AI suggestions:** a small AI model suggests where to place furniture in your space.

## The Problem

New furniture is expensive and costs trees, while usable furniture gets thrown out every time someone moves. NotIkea is a business system that helps save trees by keeping furniture in use, and it runs on a small AI model instead of a large, costly one.

## Why I Am Pursuing This

I want to make buying and sorting furniture cheaper for students and for people who care about the environment.
